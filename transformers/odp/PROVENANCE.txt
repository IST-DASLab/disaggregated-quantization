Local change: pack_layer_trained accepts validate_groups=False to skip the two
validation-only multi-projection grp() calls. Projections are still written
SEPARATELY with their ORIGINAL individual global scales. No packed codes, FP8
scales, or tensor-global scales are changed. This is only enabled explicitly
for legacy exports by the wrapper, which records incompatibilities in a manifest.

The wrapper supports text-only and multimodal tensor prefixes, explicit layer
types, staged publication, and byte-exact verification after GGUF serialization.
The standalone copy also removes the parent-repository gguf-py path injection;
it uses the gguf dependency installed from requirements.txt.
Use export_gguf.py rather than the vendored main().
